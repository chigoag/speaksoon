# Speak Soon

**Stay in touch with the people who matter — without the guilt.**

Speak Soon is a warm, private Android app that helps you keep in rhythm with family and friends. Set
how often you'd like to reach out to each person, and it sends one gentle nudge a day for whoever
you're most overdue with. No streaks, no scores, no guilt.

This repository hosts the Speak Soon **marketing site and privacy policy**, published with GitHub
Pages at **https://chigoag.github.io/speaksoon/**.

## What Speak Soon does

- **One gentle daily nudge** — never a wall of overdue tasks
- **A custom cadence for each person** — weekly, monthly, twice a year, whatever fits
- **Remember every catch-up** — log calls, texts and visits; a quick note resurfaces in your next nudge
- **Stay connected in one tap** — call or WhatsApp linked contacts straight from the app
- **A home-screen widget** showing who to reach out to next
- **Private by design** — everything stays on your phone; no account, no cloud, no tracking
- **Free for up to 10 people**; a one-time unlock gives you unlimited contacts (no subscription)

## Who it's for

Thoughtful people who want to nurture their close relationships — family, old friends, mentors — but
lose track in a busy life. It's a personal way to stay in touch, not a networking tool.
