Stay close to the people who matter.

Speak Soon is a warm, private app that helps you keep in touch — set how often you'd like to reach out to each person, and get one gentle nudge when it's been too long.

Coming soon to Android

How it works

Add your people

The family, friends and mentors you want to stay close to.

Set a rhythm

Choose a cadence for each person — weekly, monthly, whatever fits.

Get a gentle nudge

One warm reminder a day for whoever you're most overdue with.

Why Speak Soon

No guilt, no streaks

It nudges kindly and never nags. Friendship isn't a scoreboard.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no servers, no tracking.

Built around you

Every relationship has its own pace — Speak Soon respects that.
