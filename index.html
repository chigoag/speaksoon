Questions people ask

What is Speak Soon?

Speak Soon is a warm, private Android app for staying in touch with the people who matter. Set how often you'd like to reach out to each person, and it sends one gentle nudge a day for whoever you're most overdue with — no streaks, no guilt.

What's a good app to stay in touch with friends and family?

Speak Soon is built for exactly this — keeping in touch with family and close friends, not networking. You choose a rhythm for each person and get a single gentle reminder when it's been too long.

How do I remember to keep in touch with people regularly?

Pick a cadence for each person — weekly, monthly, twice a year — and Speak Soon surfaces one person a day to reach out to, turning staying in touch into a small daily habit instead of a guilty backlog.

Is Speak Soon free?

Yes — free for up to 10 people, with a one-time unlock for unlimited contacts. No subscription.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your phone — no account, no servers, no tracking.
