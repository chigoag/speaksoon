Speak Soon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

The short version

Speak Soon is built to be private by design:

  • Everything stays on your phone. Your people, notes, and contact history live only on your device.
  • No account, no servers, no cloud. There is nothing to sign up for, and we have no way to see your data.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics. We don't collect or sell anything about you.
  • You're in control. Export all your data to a file, or delete everything, at any time.

The detailed policy below explains exactly how this works and your rights under UK and EU data protection law.

1. Who this policy is for

This policy explains how the Speak Soon mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. It applies to everyone who uses the app. By using Speak Soon you accept this policy.

2. The core principle: local-first

Speak Soon is a personal reminder tool that helps you stay in contact with the people who matter to you. It is designed so that all of your information is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. The app has no user accounts and connects to no servers of ours. We, the developers, never receive, store, or have any access to your data.

3. What the app stores on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and nowhere else:

  • The people you add (names, the contact rhythm you set, and any tags).
  • Your notes and your contact history (when you logged speaking to someone, and any note you added).
  • Any photo you choose as a person's avatar.
  • If you link a person to one of your phone contacts, the single phone number you picked.
  • Your app settings (reminder time, default rhythm, and so on).

This data stays on the device until you delete it, either by removing individual items in the app or by using Settings → Your data → Delete all data.

4. What we collect and send: nothing

The app does not collect analytics, does not show adverts, contains no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs, and sends no personal data off your device. It does not even request internet access for its own features.

5. Permissions and why

  • Notifications — so your one gentle daily reminder can appear. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, or deny the permission entirely.
  • Photos you pick — when you choose an avatar, the system photo picker hands the app only the single image you selected. The app never browses your photo library.
  • Choosing a phone contact — see the next section.

The app does not ask for broad contacts access, location, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permission.

6. Linking a person to a phone contact

You can optionally link a person in Speak Soon to one of the contacts saved on your phone, so you can call or message them in one tap. When you do this, Android's own contact picker opens and you choose a single number. The app receives only that one number you picked — it is not granted permission to read your full contacts list, and it does not copy your address book. The chosen number is stored locally, like the rest of your data, and is never uploaded. You can remove the link at any time with Unlink, which also forgets the number.

7. When you tap Call or WhatsApp

If you tap Call or WhatsApp for a linked person, Speak Soon simply hands the phone number to your device's phone dialler or to WhatsApp. At that point you are using those other apps, and their own privacy policies and terms apply. Speak Soon does not place the call or send the message itself, and keeps no record of it unless you choose to log it.

8. Backups you create

You can export all of your data to a file from Settings → Your data → Export. That file is created wherever you choose to save it (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive you select). Once you have exported a file, it is under your control and outside the app — please store it somewhere you trust. You can re-import a backup file later, or import data from another contact-reminder app.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, it is kept for as long as you keep it. To erase everything, use Settings → Your data → Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes its local storage. We hold no copies, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

10. Children's privacy

Speak Soon is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from children — indeed it does not collect personal data from anyone, as all information stays on the user's own device.

11. Security

Your data is held in the app's private, sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add an extra layer by enabling App lock in Settings, which requires your fingerprint, face, or device PIN to open the app. As with any data stored on a device, keeping your phone itself secure (a screen lock, up-to-date software) is the best protection.

12. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have rights to access, correct, export, and erase your personal data. Speak Soon is designed so you can exercise these directly and immediately: you can view and edit everything in the app, export it all to a file, and delete any or all of it whenever you like. Because we never receive your data, there is no separate request to make to us.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. When a change affects how your data is handled, the app's What's new screen will flag it the next time you open an updated version, so you're not left in the dark.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email speaksoon@example.com.
